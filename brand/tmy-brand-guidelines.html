01 — Logos

The mark & wordmark

Primary lockup — maroon circle monogram + bold wordmark on baby pink.

Reversed — pink monogram + warm cream wordmark on dark ink.

Monogram only — for social avatars, favicons and small spaces.

Clear space & size

  • Keep clear space equal to half the monogram diameter on all sides.
  • Minimum monogram size: 24px / 8mm. Minimum lockup width: 140px.

Don't

  • Don't use cursive, stretch, rotate or recolour outside the palette.
  • Don't place the maroon mark on dark ink or maroon; use the reversed pink version instead.

03 — Fonts

Easy to read. Never corporate.

Display & UI

Headlines (ExtraBold 800, tight tracking), buttons and navigation (Bold 700), body (Regular 400). Google Fonts, free.

Storied accent
Aa

Instrument Serif Italic

One or two words inside a headline, in maroon, to add the "lived" feeling. Never for body copy. Google Fonts, free.

Lot tags & labels
LOT 14

System monospace

Kickers, lot numbers, karat and price tags. Uppercase with wide tracking (0.14em).

Rules: no cursive or script, nothing school-like or corporate. Hero: 92px / 0.92 line height. Body: 16–17.5px / 1.5. Kicker: 12px mono, uppercase.

04 — Brand voice

Warm, storied, a little cheeky.

Storied

Talk about where a piece has been, not just what it is. Era, karat, a detail worth noticing.

Warm & playful

Like a friend with great taste handing you something special. Short sentences. Plain words.

Honest, not salesy

No pressure, no hype, no countdown clocks. One-of-one pieces speak for themselves.

We say

Gold that's already lived a little.

"Forty-five solid-gold pieces, hand-picked from other lives and other decades. No reproductions, no two the same."

We never say

"Don't miss out! Buy now!" · "Luxury investment opportunity" · "Best-in-class quality" · "Synergy"

Style notes

  • Australian English (jewellery, colour).
  • No em dashes in copy; use full stops, commas or colons.
  • Always "Theirs. Mine. Yours." with full stops; short form "TMY".
  • Say "preloved", "solid gold", "one of one".

Real pieces, real light.

Placeholder frames: diagonal hatch with a lot tag. Replace with product photography.

Product shots

  • One piece per frame, close and sharp, on a soft coloured backdrop from the palette.
  • Natural, warm daylight. Show wear, patina and hallmarks honestly.

Lifestyle

  • Worn on real people, candid, 80s/90s boldness with French Mediterranean ease.
  • Tactile settings: linen, tiles, fruit, old books.

Avoid

  • Sterile white-box catalogue shots, heavy filters, stock photography, retouching that hides age.

07 — Graphics

Scallops, hatching & tags.

pinned from the archive ↑

Scallop wave

Section dividers and Canva backgrounds. Always true semicircles; keep proportions fixed.

Diagonal hatch

135° stripes in ink at 8% opacity, 9px on / 9px off. Fills empty photo frames and textures backgrounds.

Tilted tags & captions

Pin lot tags at small angles (±3°) with soft shadow (0 12px 30px, 16% ink). Handwritten-feel captions use Instrument Serif italic, never script.

Series order

Maroon, lime, blue, orange, yellow. Rounded bar corners (8px), thin ink axis at 20% opacity, no gridlines or 3D.

Labels

Mono type for numbers and axis labels, Bricolage Grotesque Bold for titles. Label directly on the chart where possible. The charts style is a suggested direction built from the palette.
